Procedures

Step-by-step guide to our overseas employment process from registration to successful placement.

Our Process Overview

Our systematic approach ensures a smooth and transparent process from initial registration to successful overseas placement. Each step is designed to maximize your chances of success while maintaining the highest standards of service.

8
Simple Steps
4-8
Weeks Timeline
95%
Success Rate
1

Initial Registration

1-2 Days

Register with Rising Youths Overseas and submit your basic information and documents.

Requirements:

Completed application form
Passport copy
Educational certificates
Experience certificates
2

Document Verification

3-5 Days

Our team verifies all submitted documents and conducts initial screening.

Requirements:

Original documents for verification
Police clearance certificate
Medical reports
Passport-size photographs
3

Skills Assessment

1-2 Days

Comprehensive evaluation of your skills and suitability for available positions.

Requirements:

Technical skills test
Interview session
Language proficiency test
Practical demonstration (if applicable)
4

Job Matching

1-2 Weeks

We match your profile with suitable job opportunities from our partner companies.

Requirements:

Updated CV/Resume
Preference form
Salary expectations
Location preferences
5

Employer Interview

1-2 Weeks

Interview with potential employers (may be conducted online or in-person).

Requirements:

Professional attire
Updated portfolio
Reference contacts
Interview preparation
6

Job Offer & Contract

3-7 Days

Upon successful interview, receive job offer and employment contract.

Requirements:

Contract review
Terms negotiation
Salary confirmation
Job description acceptance
7

Pre-Departure Process

2-4 Weeks

Complete all pre-departure formalities including training and documentation.

Requirements:

Visa application
Medical examination
Training completion
Travel arrangements
8

Departure & Arrival

1-2 Days

Travel to destination country with our complete support and assistance.

Requirements:

Flight tickets
Travel insurance
Emergency contacts
Arrival coordination

Important Notes

  • All documents must be original and properly attested
  • Processing times may vary depending on destination country requirements
  • Medical examinations must be conducted at approved medical centers
  • Visa processing times depend on embassy schedules and requirements
  • Training attendance is mandatory for all selected candidates
  • All fees must be paid as per the agreed payment schedule
