Ms.Prasamsha Shrestha (shreejana)

Chairman & Founder

Rising Youths Overseas

With an unwavering passion for transforming lives through international opportunities, Shreejana founded Rising Youths Overseas with a simple yet powerful vision: to bridge the gap between talented Nepali professionals and global career prospects. Her 17+ years journey in the recruitment industry began with personally experiencing the challenges of overseas employment, which ignited her mission to create a company that genuinely cares about each individual's dreams and aspirations. Known for her hands-on approach and personal involvement in every placement, she has successfully guided over 1,000 professionals to achieve their international career goals. Her leadership philosophy centers on treating every client as family, ensuring they receive not just job placements, but comprehensive support for their entire journey abroad.

Prabin DK

CEO

Rising Youths Overseas

A dynamic leader with an exceptional ability to streamline complex processes and deliver results that exceed expectations, Prabin brings over a decade of experience in project management and international business operations. His analytical mind and attention to detail ensure that every recruitment process runs seamlessly from initial consultation to successful placement. Known for his innovative approach to solving challenges, he has pioneered several digital solutions that have revolutionized how Rising Youths Overseas manages client relationships and tracks placement success. Prabin's commitment to excellence is reflected in his hands-on management style, where he personally oversees critical projects and maintains direct communication with both clients and international partners. His vision for the future includes expanding Rising Youths' digital footprint while maintaining the personal touch that sets the company apart.

Hem Raj Koirala

Executive Director

Rising Youths Overseas

A passionate advocate for worker rights and workplace equality, Hem Raj brings a wealth of experience from his previous roles in labor unions and human rights organizations. His deep understanding of international labor laws and regulations makes him an invaluable asset to both the company and the workers it serves. Having personally witnessed the challenges faced by overseas workers, he has dedicated his career to ensuring fair treatment, proper compensation, and safe working conditions for all placements. His empathetic approach and excellent communication skills in multiple languages help him connect with workers from diverse backgrounds, providing them with the guidance and support they need throughout their overseas journey. Hem Raj's commitment goes beyond just legal compliance – he actively works to build long-term relationships with international employers to create sustainable, ethical employment opportunities that benefit both workers and companies.

Anil Ale Magar

General Manager

Rising Youths Overseas

With an MBA in International Business and over 12 years of experience in human resource management, Anil brings strategic leadership and operational excellence to Rising Youths Overseas. His expertise in talent acquisition, organizational development, and cross-cultural communication has been instrumental in building strong partnerships with international employers across 15+ countries. Known for his data-driven approach and innovative recruitment strategies, he has successfully implemented modern HR technologies that have improved placement efficiency by 300%. Anil's bilingual abilities and cultural sensitivity make him particularly effective in bridging communication gaps between Nepali candidates and international employers. His leadership style emphasizes team collaboration and continuous learning, fostering an environment where every team member can contribute to the company's mission of transforming lives through global opportunities. Beyond his professional achievements, Anil is passionate about mentoring young professionals and regularly conducts career development workshops.

Mr. Dhurba Kumar Shrestha

International Managing Director

Rising Youths Overseas

It is my pleasure to welcome you to Rising Youths Overseas Pvt. Ltd., a trusted partner in international manpower recruitment. Our mission is to connect skilled and capable Nepalese workers with reputable employers around the world. We believe that overseas employment is not just about providing jobs—it is about creating opportunities that transform lives.

At Rising Youths Overseas, we maintain the highest standards of integrity, transparency, and professionalism in every step of our process. From candidate sourcing and selection to documentation and final deployment, we ensure every detail is handled with precision. Our experienced team works diligently to understand employer requirements and match them with the most suitable candidates.

We prioritize ethical recruitment practices to safeguard the rights and welfare of all our candidates. Safety, legality, and dignity are the pillars of our employment services. We also provide comprehensive pre-departure training and orientation to prepare candidates for global work environments. Our global partners rely on us for timely, reliable, and efficient workforce solutions across multiple industries.

We are committed to continuous improvement, adopting best practices and international standards in recruitment. Collaboration, trust, and long-term relationships are the foundation of our work with employers and candidates alike. We strive to empower Nepalese youth, enabling them to achieve their professional goals abroad. As International Managing Director, I am proud of our team's dedication and the success stories we have created together.
