License & Certificates

Our official licenses and certifications that demonstrate our commitment to legal and ethical practices.

Renewal Certificate (Nepal Notary Public Council)

Date of Renewal: 30-07-2025

Validity: Up to mid-July 2026

Details: This certificate confirms the renewal of our operational license by the Nepal Notary Public Council. It includes strict compliance terms, such as restrictions on establishing branch offices without prior approval, prohibition on working through unauthorized agents, and mandatory adherence to Nepal Government directives. The document is authenticated with the official seal and signature of the Section Officer, ensuring its legal validity.

Foreign Employment License

License No: 1783/082/083

Date: 2082/04/14

Issued To: Rising Youths Overseas Pvt. Ltd.

Details: Issued by the Ministry of Labor, Employment and Social Security, Government of Nepal, this license authorizes Rising Youths Overseas Pvt. Ltd. to provide foreign employment services under the Foreign Employment Act 2064 (2007 AD). It includes regulatory terms ensuring compliance with national laws, signed by Director Tikaram Dhakal, and verified with the official seal and notary certification for authenticity.

Nepali Renewal Record

Date of Renewal: 2082/04/14 B.S. (30-07-2025 AD)

Validity: Up to Ashadh 2083 B.S. (mid-July 2026)

Details: This official renewal logbook entry, written in Nepali, records the license renewal with handwritten notes, an official seal, and the signature of the renewal officer. It explicitly prohibits opening branch offices, working through agents, sending workers to unpermitted countries, and mandates strict compliance with government regulations, ensuring our operations align with legal standards.

Our Commitment to Compliance

Rising Youths Overseas Pvt. Ltd. is fully licensed and certified to operate as a manpower agency in Nepal. We maintain all necessary licenses and certifications to ensure that our operations are legal, ethical, and in compliance with both Nepali and international regulations.

Our certifications include:

  • Valid license from the Department of Foreign Employment, Government of Nepal
  • ISO 9001:2015 certification for quality management systems
  • Active membership in the Nepal Association of Foreign Employment Agencies (NAFEA)
  • Proper business registration and tax compliance
  • Insurance coverage for our operations and client protection

We regularly renew our licenses and certifications to ensure continuous compliance with evolving regulations and industry standards. This commitment to legal and ethical practices is fundamental to our mission of providing trustworthy manpower services.
